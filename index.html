Propositional Logic

Truth table generator

A proposition is a statement that is either true or false. Combine p, q, r, s with connectives and the table lists every possible case, one column per sub-expression.

In ITEvery if (loggedIn && !banned) is a compound proposition, and the logic gates inside a CPU compute exactly these truth tables.

Typing works too: ~ or ! not, & and, | or, -> implies, <-> iff, xor.

Matrices

Matrix calculator

Enter two matrices and pick an operation. Click any cell of the answer to see exactly how it was computed. Fractions like 1/2 and decimals work, and answers stay exact.

In ITDigital images are matrices of pixel values, neural networks multiply matrices of weights, and 3D engines move every vertex with a matrix product.

Matrices in Graphics

2×2 transformations

A 2×2 matrix moves every point of the plane. Its first column is where î = (1, 0) lands and its second column is where ĵ = (0, 1) lands. The determinant is how much areas are scaled.

In ITRotating a phone screen, resizing an image or skewing text with CSS transform: matrix() all come down to this multiplication.
